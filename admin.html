CampusEats Analytics Documentation & Calculation Methodology

Collected Revenue (Net)

Calculated as ∑(Order Total) across filtered orders. Represents the actual revenue collected after applying volume discounts.

Total Order Value (Gross)

Calculated as ∑(Order Subtotal) before discounts. Represents the full menu value of all food items ordered.

Average Order Value (AOV)

Calculated dynamically as Collected Revenue ÷ Total Orders. Safely yields ₹0 when no orders exist.

Volume-Based Discounts

Preserves CampusEats tiered rules: Below ₹100 (0%), ₹100–₹199 (10%), ₹200–₹299 (15%), ₹300–₹499 (20%), ₹500+ (25%). Derived from saved order totals.
